# European Banking Customer Churn Analytics

## Project Overview
This project analyzes customer churn patterns in European banking using customer segmentation and exploratory data analysis. The objective is to identify customer groups with higher churn risk and generate actionable business insights for customer retention.

## Business Objectives
- Analyze the overall customer churn rate.
- Identify churn patterns across customer segments.
- Compare churn across France, Germany, and Spain.
- Analyze customer churn based on age, credit score, tenure, balance, and activity status.
- Identify high-risk customer segments and provide business recommendations.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- Tableau
- Microsoft Excel

## Key Insights
- Overall customer churn rate: 20.4%
- Germany recorded the highest churn rate at 32.44%.
- Customers aged 46–60 recorded the highest churn rate at 51.1%.
- Inactive customers showed a churn rate of 26.85%.
- High-balance customers recorded a churn rate of 23.12%.
- Customer activity was one of the important indicators associated with churn.

## Customer Segmentation
Customers were segmented based on:
- Geography
- Age Group
- Credit Score Group
- Tenure Group
- Balance Group
- Activity Status

## Dashboard
The Tableau dashboard provides interactive analysis of customer churn across different customer segments using KPI cards, filters, and segment-level visualizations.

### Dashboard KPIs
- Overall Churn Rate
- Segment Churn Rate
- High-Value Churn Ratio
- Engagement Drop Indicator

## Business Recommendations
The analysis suggests prioritizing retention initiatives for high-risk customer groups, particularly middle-aged customers, inactive customers, customers in Germany, and customers with significant account balances. Customer engagement programs and targeted retention strategies can help reduce churn risk.
